# Truth-table recovery and bounded refinement

Start with `recover_truth.py input.truth final.v --budget-seconds <available-time> --json --brief`. It snapshots each proposal in a unique attempt directory, runs exact frontend/domain validation, and atomically commits a first verified output. Its local receipt binds the truth table, final bytes and cost policy. Refinement uses `--candidate proposal.v`; a failed attempt preserves the existing output. The receipt is workflow evidence, not a substitute for independent final evaluation.

## Semantic recognition

Interpret `read_truth -xf` with complemented input indexing and line j as output y[j]. Infer words, flags, packing and guards under that contract. Recognizers test arithmetic, comparisons, projection/affine structure, reductions, encoders, format conversions and floating-point operations against the complete table. A family label is a hypothesis until emitted RTL passes the same complete-domain check.

For floating point, identify the input/output format, operation, rounding, DAZ/FTZ behavior, canonical NaN, infinity, signed zero and saturation. Build field decode, range reduction, bounded fixed-point or integer iteration, normalization and rounding. Prefer arithmetic recurrences and analytically derived coefficients over repeated hand-written terms. Integer widths and finite-precision order matter: a mathematically equivalent series can round differently. Validate every generated precision/degree choice against all table entries, including exceptional values and rounding boundaries.

`truth_describe.py` is useful for diagnosing an unmatched table. Avoid calling it separately when a completed recovery report already identifies the semantic family. Existing `truth_reverse.py` remains available for candidate exploration or directory generation; its generated file is not a final checkpoint until exact validation and selection complete.

## Time and candidate retention

Use the task's total wall budget, including inference and all tool calls. Under a 600-second task, a first recovery budget of 240 seconds leaves time for a short refinement and the final response. These are configurable limits, not a reason to discard a good candidate at 240 seconds.

Keep a final implementation immediately after its first exact check. The default policy allows at most two further proposals and 120 seconds after the first checkpoint is published, counting wall time between commands; two consecutive non-improvements stop refinement. The journal is stored beside the output and binds the immutable first receipt. Each attempt is reserved before inference, pricing or proof, and failures/timeouts/crashes consume it. Initial policy flags may shrink these limits; later CLI calls cannot reset or change them. A missing or contradictory journal fails closed while preserving the verified output. Keep both first and selected checkpoint evidence in the final report. Cost-aware promotion never overwrites a verified file with an unverified proposal. Avoid sequential invocations of generation-with-verification, a second direct verifier and a third generic checker on unchanged bytes. A changed candidate requires a new check; a local content/tool-bound cache only reuses the same complete proof. External experiment coordinators must bypass local caches.

The tool removes write permission from the selected output while retaining its read permissions. Successful promotions use atomic replacement, so read-only output does not prevent verified improvement. Do not change these permissions, edit or manually replace the selected output, or copy in a separately checked proposal after the journal has closed. End with `recover_truth.py input.truth final.v --check-checkpoint --json --brief`: `checkpoint_validated` binds the actual final bytes to the current complete receipt and canonical budget without generation, another proof, another proposal or journal mutation. Pending interrupted promotions require normal recovery first. An unbound final is a failed obligation, not a verified result.

## Representation and coverage

Truth-derived lookup arrays, flat input case enumerations and field LUTs are excluded unless the user explicitly permits them. Exhaustive exploration, table-driven testbenches and checking all assignments are allowed: they support validation rather than final implementation.

When complete exact coverage is requested, `--allow-boolean-fallback` enables shared ANF/ROBDD after semantic recognition fails. Such a result is low-level and cannot be called recovered word-level RTL. The final checker must establish both frontend acceptance and all-input/all-output equivalence; unsupported grammar, unknown bits and incomplete coverage remain inconclusive. A generic bit-parallel evaluator may establish exact Boolean behavior efficiently without simulating one vector at a time. Unsupported lowered cells use interleaved exhaustive Icarus replay: one worker below 12 input bits, four otherwise, under one shared replay deadline. Its certificate binds all residue-class partitions, complete vector counts, the truth table, lowered RTL, checker and worker command logs; no partial worker success is a complete proof. It must evaluate the actual lowered graph and complete interface, not merely a convenient subset of source assignments.

## Netlist proof invariants

For primitive netlists, see [exact CEC](exact_cec.md). Preserve every original output and bridge. In `auto`, conclusive engine disagreement is conflict; a killed or timed-out attempt cannot be accepted from partial success text. SMT requires lowering generalized checks, auditing every serialized output equality and assertion enable, and proving the all-output miter. Independent output-bit SAT must cover all bits; lemma chains may assume only earlier proved equalities. Algebraic or cofactor certificates require independent full replay, supported native widths and complete branch coverage. Unsupported constructs fail closed.


## Endpoint guards and Boolean construction

For a matched floating-point family, a few broad normal-magnitude identity or constant endpoint regions can bypass an expensive core. Infer each continuous region from the supplied oracle, require a large minimum coverage and a small region count, and exclude NaN/Inf and DAZ handling from that inference. Do not add isolated input-answer patches. Every changed candidate still needs exact verification over the complete domain. A source-level early return can become an output mux after Yosys lowering while leaving arithmetic driven by the original input. Explicitly mux the whole core operand to a safe fixed value in bypass regions, keep original input fields for externally visible guards, and inspect the lowered dependencies before claiming a replay improvement. Source proofs and timeout records from rejected attempts remain separate.

The exact Boolean generator uses scalar nodes and compares ordinary and complement-edge BDDs, bounded input-variable orders, and factored ANF. It can group output functions only after checking their input-permutation relation against the complete truth table, then emit a reusable Boolean helper with explicit scalar computations. Source-function reuse does not establish physical sharing. The active cost CSV decides candidate selection; raw nodes, monomials or source bytes alone are insufficient. These are low-level representations even when a helper packs several output bits. They may establish V when explicitly permitted but never H. Frontend acceptance and complete replay remain required after choosing a route.

Procedural Boolean helpers are admitted only within fixed work bounds: at most 4096 scalar assignments per helper and 32768 assignments after accounting for distinct argument permutations. Larger helpers retain the exact flat scalar DAG alternatives. Unused procedural scratch aliases can make the strict bit-parallel checker return unsupported and require exhaustive simulation, so a lower source cost alone does not justify bypassing these bounds or weakening the checker.

For finite floating-point numeric-code searches, derive adjacent-code midpoint formulas from the format's dyadic spacing, including subnormal and binade boundaries. A doubled-magnitude decoder can compute both input squares and squared adjacent midpoints without a separate midpoint addition or factor-of-four shift. Compose helpers whose intermediate result is otherwise unused, retain full decoded/product/sum widths, and preserve parity rounding, saturation and NaN guards; verify the complete domain before selecting the rewrite.

The syntax-cost calculator distinguishes packed selections from unpacked array elements. Its lexical parsing remains approximate for parameter expressions and scopes; use an independently bound scorer implementation and the active weight CSV. A changed calculator requires fresh cost measurements and a new version record. Do not substitute rescored values into an older experiment without marking the change.

Shared static input fields and complete fixed-width rounding helpers reduce repeated semantic syntax. Compact square, power-quotient and root algorithms keep finite-precision normalization, midpoint tests and exceptional values explicit. A simplified Python identity is not sufficient: generated RTL must pass full-domain checks, including signed zero, subnormals, saturation and ties. The cost walker descends into concatenations and counts their internal operations; concatenation component charges retain the configured counting rule. Do not rely on declaration spelling or expression containers to hide computation from the scorer.

Fixed shift amounts permit compile-time calculation of a rounding bias; the shared helper still uses a widened signed sum, retains ties-to-even and applies each intermediate and final rounding in order. Rewriting exponent packing must reject unsupported uses of the exponent variable. Helpers with signed 64-bit ports must not capture a wider unsigned reduction or change its truncation, even when the surrounding polynomial uses signed 64-bit words.

Dynamic signed-64 RNE can use the same widened helper when both preceding paths definitely assign a shift in 1..63. Derive the bias as `2**(shift-1)-1` and retain the original source bit at that shift. Parse the complete procedural body, preserve each rounding stage, and reject unknown scratch uses, unsupported syntax or unproved shift bounds. Compare the fully compacted alternatives with the active CSV before choosing one.

A staged cubic root can compare powered midpoint intervals at the final precision while preserving the requested intermediate RNE. The boundary moves in opposite directions for even and odd lower output codes; direct rounding can differ from two successive roundings. A complete format-wide significand/exponent-residue proof may reduce the intermediate working precision only when all staged outputs stay identical. Preserve DAZ, signed zero, infinity and canonical NaN; retain the original algorithm if the bounded format contract is unsupported. The current numeric scan supports at most ten fraction bits and 1024 intervals, and still requires the unchanged frontend and complete-domain replay. Source reuse does not establish smaller synthesized hardware.

For fused finite packed floating-point dots, decode lanes into their common integer unit and derive signed decode, product and sum widths from the largest format value and lane count. The compact encoder is admitted only when every reachable nonzero sum falls in the output's normal finite range; otherwise retain subnormal and saturation handling. Apply RNE once to the exact fused sum and allow a rounding carry to enter the packed exponent. Cancellation produces positive zero; an all-zero reduction is negative only when every product has negative sign. Check these contracts mathematically and replay the complete input domain before selection.

Small packed Q1.1 reductions can accumulate lanes, normalize their integer half-units and apply the sole required RNE shift without a field lookup. Small integer square roots also admit ascending numeric search or monotone arithmetic descent. In descent, initialize `y` to the largest representable root and repeat `y = y - (y*y > radicand)` at most that many times. Each step either decreases an overestimate or retains the exact floor root. The unsigned comparison context must be twice the root width, so odd input widths cannot truncate the square. These alternatives are capped at 16 input bits and 256 trials and compete with the bit-prefix source under the active CSV. Their source can be shorter while their unrolled circuit is larger; this comparison establishes source cost only. Wider or differently packed outputs retain the established bit-prefix algorithm.

Signed-width operator factoring compares fully rendered alternatives. Count uses in the original kernel: leaving a single-use word operation directly visible can avoid a helper return-range charge. Preserve operand signedness, the intermediate width and truncation; scalar predicate helpers and protected normalization loops retain their established contracts. A lower preliminary score must not remove an alternative before later rounding and field sharing are applied. The active CSV selects between complete candidates, and the original frontend and complete-domain checker decide whether the selected representation is valid.

For finite-only binary hypot with at most three fraction bits, begin at the larger magnitude encoding and test a format-derived bound of adjacent squared midpoints. Widen decoded squares and their sum. Strict comparison implements RNE only when a format-wide lattice argument rules out ties with an odd retained code; otherwise retain the parity condition. Keep the sole NaN payload and finite saturation explicit. This route is inapplicable to a format that reserves infinity or multiple NaN payloads.

For a periodic kernel using an odd inverse-pi integer constant, multiplication preserves the hidden significand's two-adic valuation. If every normal exponent's midpoint has a larger valuation, quotient ties are impossible. The nearest quotient may then use `(product + half) >> shift`, with one extra bit in the sum; neither the product nor its precision may be shortened. Compute the signed fractional residual from the difference of the full shifted product and quotient at the fixed binary point. Removing unsigned zero-padding is valid only when the assignment context still gives multiplication its original full width.

Endpoint compaction can share a sign-selected small-region threshold and a sign-selected saturation threshold between output guards and core-operand isolation. Derive these unions from the already matched continuous regions, preserve special-value output priority, and validate the exact typed helper definitions before rewriting. Preserve the original finite numerical kernel and every intermediate rounding. A new guard must not activate an expensive kernel on inputs previously isolated without checking complete replay under the unchanged deadline.
